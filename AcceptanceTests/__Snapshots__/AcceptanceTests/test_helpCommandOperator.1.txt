OVERVIEW: Describes a given mutation operator.

USAGE: swift-mutator operator <operator>

ARGUMENTS:
  <operator>                  Available operators are:
                          RelationalOperatorReplacement, RelationalBoundary,
                          RemoveSideEffects, ChangeLogicalConnector,
                          SwapTernary, NegateConditional,
                          ChangeArithmeticOperator, FlipBooleanLiteral
                              For all operators use: all

OPTIONS:
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.
